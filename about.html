Who We Are

Built on Three Generations of Trust

Shri Mekalsuta Traders was founded with a simple belief: quality materials and honest service build lasting relationships. Our legacy was established by our founder, Late Shri Nathuram Ji Verma, who believed that trust is the foundation of every great business.

Located in Bareli, Madhya Pradesh, we have grown from a local building materials supplier to a trusted, multi-brand authorized dealer serving contractors, civil engineers, architects, industrial buyers, government contractors and home owners across the region.

Today, we are proud authorized dealers of UltraTech Cement, Tata Steel, JSW Steel, Kamdhenu, Jindal Steel, APL Apollo, Tata Structura and many more. We source TMT bars directly from Raipur to ensure the best quality at the most competitive prices.
